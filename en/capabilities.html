Credentials

Certifications

Inertia Guangzhou is certified to ISO 13485:2016 for medical device quality management systems (identical to the Chinese standard GB/T 42061-2022), Certificate No. HG25MD0030R0S, issued by Crown Certification & Inspection Co., Ltd., valid through 2028-10-22, covering the sale and export of mobility aid components. Building on this system, Inertia Guangzhou provides manufacturing collaboration, quality control, and supply chain management services for regulated medical devices.

Inspection & Testing

Inspection Capabilities

44 calibrated instruments across five testing dimensions

  • Dimensional & geometric: calipers, ID/OD micrometers, pin gauges, height gauges, feeler gauges, taper gauges, ultrasonic thickness, runout measurement
  • Mechanical & assembly control: Shore hardness, push/pull force, spring tension, torque testing and torque-tool calibration
  • Appearance & surface: color difference, gloss, coating/film thickness, light transmittance, luminous flux
  • Environmental & reliability: salt spray, drying/temperature control, infrared temperature measurement
  • Battery & others: 8-channel battery test system, electronic counting scale

Representative instruments: outside micrometer (0–25 mm / 0.01 mm), digital inside micrometer (5–30 mm), colorimeter (3nh NH310), torque tester (SN-20B), film thickness gauge (VC852B), salt spray tester (Lionde 60A), 8-channel battery test system (CT-4008Q).
